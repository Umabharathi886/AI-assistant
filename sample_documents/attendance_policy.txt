NovaTech Solutions Pvt. Ltd. — Attendance Policy

1. Working Hours
Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday through Friday,
with a one-hour lunch break. Core hours (10:00 AM – 4:00 PM) require
all employees to be online or in-office.

2. Attendance Tracking
Attendance is tracked automatically via the company's biometric system
(office) or the timesheet portal (remote/WFH days).

3. Late Arrivals
Employees arriving more than 15 minutes after core hours begin on more
than 3 occasions in a month will receive a notice from their manager.

4. Minimum Attendance Requirement
Employees must maintain at least 90% monthly attendance (accounting
for approved leave) to remain eligible for the quarterly attendance
bonus.

5. Attendance Percentage Calculation
Attendance % = (Days Present / Total Working Days in Period) x 100

6. Irregular Attendance
Three or more unexplained absences in a rolling 30-day period will
trigger an HR review meeting.
