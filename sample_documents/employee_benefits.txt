NovaTech Solutions Pvt. Ltd. — Employee Benefits

1. Health Insurance
Group medical insurance covering the employee, spouse, and up to 2
dependent children, with coverage of INR 5,00,000 per family per year.

2. Retirement Benefits
Provident Fund (PF) contribution as per statutory norms, plus an
optional National Pension Scheme (NPS) matching contribution up to 5%
of basic salary.

3. Wellness Benefits
- Annual health checkup reimbursement up to INR 5,000
- Subsidized gym membership at partner fitness centers
- Free access to the Employee Assistance Program (confidential
  counseling and mental health support)

4. Learning & Development
Annual learning budget of INR 20,000 per employee for courses,
certifications, or conference attendance, subject to manager approval.

5. Other Perks
- Meal allowance
- Annual performance bonus (variable, based on rating and company
  performance)
- Referral bonus for successful hires
- Employee stock option plan (ESOP) for eligible roles/levels
