NovaTech Solutions Pvt. Ltd. — IT Security Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

1. Password Policy
Passwords must be at least 12 characters, include upper/lowercase,
numbers, and symbols, and be rotated every 90 days. Reuse of the last
5 passwords is not permitted.

2. VPN Usage
All access to internal systems from outside the office network must
go through the company VPN. Public Wi-Fi must never be used to access
sensitive systems without VPN enabled.

3. Device Security
Company devices must have full-disk encryption, an approved antivirus
agent, and automatic screen lock after 5 minutes of inactivity.

4. Incident Reporting
Any suspected security incident (phishing email, lost device,
unauthorized access) must be reported to IT Support within 1 hour via
the #it-security-incidents channel or security@novatech.example.

5. Data Classification
Company data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or
Restricted. Confidential and Restricted data must never be shared
outside approved, encrypted channels.

6. Access Reviews
IT Support conducts quarterly access reviews to ensure employees only
retain system access relevant to their current role.
