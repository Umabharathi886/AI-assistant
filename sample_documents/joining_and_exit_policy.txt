NovaTech Solutions Pvt. Ltd. — Joining Process & Exit Policy

PART A: JOINING PROCESS
1. Offer & Onboarding Kit
Upon offer acceptance, HR sends an onboarding kit including the offer
letter, ID proof checklist, and Day-1 schedule.

2. Day 1
New hires complete IT asset issuance (laptop, credentials), HR
documentation, and a welcome session with their reporting manager.

3. Probation Period
All new employees undergo a 3-month probation period, during which
performance and fit are reviewed at the 6-week and 12-week marks.

4. Buddy Program
Each new hire is assigned a peer "buddy" for their first 30 days to
help with onboarding questions and team integration.

PART B: EXIT POLICY
1. Notice Period
Standard notice period is 60 days for confirmed employees and 15 days
during probation, unless otherwise specified in the employment
contract.

2. Exit Formalities
Employees must complete the exit checklist: knowledge transfer, asset
return (laptop, ID card), and a exit interview with HR.

3. Full & Final Settlement
Final settlement (including pending salary, leave encashment, and
reimbursements) is processed within 45 days of the last working day.

4. Relieving Letter & Experience Certificate
Issued within 7 working days after full & final settlement is
completed, provided all exit formalities are cleared.
