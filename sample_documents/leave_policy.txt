NovaTech Solutions Pvt. Ltd. — Leave Policy

1. Annual Leave
Every full-time employee is entitled to 18 paid annual leave days per
calendar year, accrued at 1.5 days per month. Unused leave up to 5 days
may be carried forward to the next year; any excess is forfeited.

2. Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick leave days per year. A medical
certificate is required for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days.

3. Casual Leave
6 casual leave days per year are provided for short-notice personal
matters. Casual leave cannot be combined with annual leave for more
than 4 consecutive days without manager approval.

4. Maternity & Paternity Leave
Maternity leave: 26 weeks paid, as per statutory requirements.
Paternity leave: 2 weeks paid, to be taken within 3 months of the
child's birth or adoption.

5. Leave Request Process
All leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 3
working days in advance (except sick leave). Requests are approved by
the employee's direct manager and copied to HR for record-keeping.

6. Unpaid Leave
Employees may request unpaid leave of up to 30 days per year, subject
to manager and HR approval, for extenuating circumstances.

7. Public Holidays
Employees are entitled to all public holidays listed in the annual
Holiday List published by HR each January.
