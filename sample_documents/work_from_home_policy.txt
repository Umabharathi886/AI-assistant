NovaTech Solutions Pvt. Ltd. — Work From Home (WFH) Policy

1. Eligibility
All full-time employees who have completed their 3-month probation
period are eligible to work from home, subject to manager approval and
role suitability (client-facing/on-site roles may have restrictions).

2. WFH Allowance
Employees may work from home up to 8 days per month. Additional WFH
days require prior written approval from the department head.

3. Equipment & Connectivity
Employees must have a stable internet connection (minimum 20 Mbps) and
use company-issued or approved equipment with VPN access enabled for
all work-related activity.

4. Availability
WFH employees must remain reachable during core hours (10:00 AM –
4:00 PM) via Slack/Teams and attend all scheduled meetings on video.

5. Full Remote Arrangements
Permanent full-remote arrangements are evaluated case-by-case by HR
and the relevant department head, typically for specialized roles or
approved relocation situations.

6. Data Security
Employees working remotely must follow the IT Security SOP, including
locking devices when unattended and never using public Wi-Fi for
sensitive company data without VPN.
